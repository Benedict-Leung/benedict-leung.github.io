Nice to meet you

Hi, I'm Ben.

Benedict, if we're being formal. I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at Ontario Tech University.

I design interactions for the pen, the eye, and the mind.

My research is in human–computer interaction. I focus on novel ways to interact with hardware like pen-based devices, eye trackers, and brain–computer interfaces.

Lately, I design how people work with AI.

My recent research creates new interactions with large language models, from AI that annotates documents alongside your pen to questions generated from where you look.

In third grade, I wanted to be an inventor.

I wanted to create something never made before, and use it to help people who couldn't do a specific task.

Then I discovered programming.

I was fascinated that you can put your heart and soul into a program and have it do exactly what you envisioned. Your legacy can live on in your programs for generations.

I want my legacy to reach people worldwide, like aiding the blind or advancing neural interfaces.

Someone must take the first step.

These dreams may seem distant. I'll take that step, even if no one else is willing.
